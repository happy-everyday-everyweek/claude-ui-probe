Trimmed Math: what was changed from Latin Modern Math
=====================================================

Trimmed Math, the font in trimmed-math.otf in this folder, is not the
original Latin Modern Math. It is a copy of version 1.959 of that font,
changed by Anthropic in October and November 2026 as set out below, and
renamed as that font's licence asks. The authors of Latin Modern Math,
B. Jackowski, P. Strzelczyk and P. Pianowski, took no part in the changes.
Please do not report a fault in this copy to them. Latin Modern Math's
licence and its authors' description are beside it, in
GUST-FONT-LICENSE.txt and README-of-original-Latin-Modern-Math.txt.

What was removed:
- 2,040 of the font's 4,802 glyphs, the ones AndroidMath can never draw:
  - 1,990 forms drawn for superscripts and subscripts, and 20 dotless
    forms. The font offers these only through its OpenType substitution
    features, and AndroidMath applies none.
  - 30 other glyphs that no character, no substitution rule and no list of
    larger forms in the MATH table leads to.
  The last eight glyph numbers belonged to removed glyphs and are gone, so
  the font now counts 4,794 glyphs. The other 2,032 removed glyphs keep
  their numbers and names as empty glyphs of no width, so that no
  remaining glyph changes its number.
- With those glyphs, what the font's other tables said of them: in the
  MATH table 338 italic corrections, 1,317 accent positions and 12
  extended-shape marks, which between them belong to 1,434 of the removed
  glyphs; in the GDEF table their glyph classes; in the CFF table 482 of
  the 1,039 subroutines that glyph programs share. The 557 subroutines
  that remain were renumbered.
- The substitution rules themselves: the GSUB table's three features
  (aalt, dtls, ssty), their three lookups, and the default language
  system of its one script record, which listed the three features.
- The ten name records and the one character map in the Macintosh
  encoding. The ten Windows name records and the four Unicode character
  maps remain. Apart from what is described under "What else was
  changed", the Windows records hold the same text as the removed ones,
  the copyright notice included.
- The FFTM table, which holds time stamps of the FontForge editor.
- Two parts that held nothing: the GDEF table's ligature-caret list and
  the GPOS table's one script record.

What else was changed:
- The font's names and the files' names: Latin Modern Math's licence asks,
  without requiring it, that a changed copy be given new ones. The family
  name "Latin Modern Math" is now "Trimmed Math", and the full and
  PostScript names "LatinModernMath-Regular" are now "TrimmedMath-Regular",
  both in the name records and in the CFF table. The file
  latinmodern-math.otf is now trimmed-math.otf, and the authors'
  README-Latin-Modern-Math.txt, with its text unchanged, is now
  README-of-original-Latin-Modern-Math.txt.
- Two name records, the version string and the unique font identifier,
  end with "; modified by Anthropic (unused glyphs removed), see
  CHANGES-Latin-Modern-Math.txt", so that the font itself says it is a
  changed copy. The font's style name and its copyright and trademark
  notices are as the authors wrote them.
- The "last modified" date in the font is set to 1980-01-01 (it was
  2014-09-05).
- Three entries of the CFF table that hold their format's default values
  are no longer written out: Encoding 0, BlueShift 7 and ForceBold 0.

What is unchanged: every character the original font maps, every taller,
wider or pieced-together form that its MATH table names, the outline, width
and hinting of every glyph that remains, each glyph's number, the font's
style name, and its version number.

How: with the subsetter of fontTools (https://github.com/fonttools/fonttools),
asked for every character of the font and every name ID, with no layout
features and with glyph numbers retained. Its defaults also drop the
Macintosh name records and character map. The new names and the ending
quoted above were then written, also with fontTools.

README-of-original-Latin-Modern-Math.txt says the work consists of the
files listed in MANIFEST-Latin-Modern-Math.txt. That manifest belongs to
the original distribution; AndroidMath never shipped it and it is not in
this folder.

The original, unmodified font, with its manifest and documentation:
https://www.gust.org.pl/projects/e-foundry/lm-math
(also on CTAN: https://ctan.org/tex-archive/fonts/lm-math)
